TECHNOLOGY THAT MAKES A DIFFERENCE

Light of true value, built on technology and quality

Every installation environment is different, and so is the technology applied. The points below apply only to the products for which the specification is stated in our catalog.

  1. 01

    Low-power operation

    Low-power operation and an energy-saving function provide a long service life.

    Applies toØ138 road stud, battery and hybrid types — up to 3 years of use

  2. 02

    Solar & hybrid power

    Solar and hybrid (solar + battery) power options allow easy installation without wiring.

    Applies toPark security lights, recessed floor lights, fence post lights, accent lighting — 5 days or more without sunlight

  3. 03

    Shockproof, waterproof, dustproof

    Ingress protection is designed per product for road and outdoor environments.

    Applies toIP68: road studs (Ø138, replacement module), curb stone lighting, recessed floor lights, fence post lights / IP65: logo projector, park light, bollard light

  4. 04

    Replaceable module structure

    Only the internal module is replaced while the fixture stays in place, reducing maintenance costs.

    Applies toØ138 LED road stud and replacement modules

  5. 05

    Lighting tailored to the site

    Custom lengths, adjustable angles, bracket options and color effects to suit site conditions.

    Applies toLED linear light, LED linear floodlight, LED circle floodlight

  6. 06

    Direct production & quality control

    Manufacturing and quality are managed in-house, backed by factory registration, direct production certificates and a dedicated R&D department.

    Applies toDirect production certificates: road studs, LED landscape lighting fixtures
