CEO MESSAGE

Safety Illuminated by Light, Urban Excellence Elevated by Technology

Greetings,

GreenOne Co., Ltd. is a specialized manufacturer of road studs and landscape lighting, dedicated to creating safer roads for people and more beautiful nightscapes for cities.

We understand that even a small road marker or a single light illuminating a street can help protect someone's precious everyday life and shape the overall impression of a city. With this responsibility in mind, we have gone beyond simply manufacturing lighting structures, continuously pursuing rigorous quality control and technological innovation to create "Light of True Value."

Combining the meticulous perspective of a women-owned business with an unwavering commitment to technical craftsmanship, we strive to provide optimal solutions tailored to the ever-changing urban environment. Our catalog reflects both our confidence in our technology and our sincere commitment to our customers.

As your most trusted partner in light and safety, GreenOne Co., Ltd. will always be by your side.

Thank you.

GreenOne Co., Ltd. CEOMoon Ji-won
