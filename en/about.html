ABOUT GREENONE

Safety Illuminated by Light, Urban Excellence Elevated by Technology

GreenOne Co., Ltd. is a specialized manufacturer of road studs and landscape lighting, dedicated to creating safer roads for people and more beautiful nightscapes for cities.

We have gone beyond simply manufacturing lighting structures, continuously pursuing rigorous quality control and technological innovation to create "Light of True Value."

Combining the meticulous perspective of a women-owned business with an unwavering commitment to technical craftsmanship, we provide products and solutions tailored to the ever-changing urban environment.

ROAD STUDRoad StudBattery, solar, hybrid and mains-powered LED road studs, plus replacement modules for the Ø138 stud.LED LANDSCAPE LIGHTINGLED Landscape LightingLinear lights, floodlights, in-ground lights, wall lights, park lights and more — a wide range of fixtures for the urban nightscape.
